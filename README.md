🌐 **Website:** [unfollowig.netlify.app](http://unfollowig.netlify.app)

Find out who doesn't follow you back on Instagram.  
Open source. No login. No external apps.

## Stack

- Next.js 16 + TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- next-intl (EN/ES)
- pnpm

## How It Works

1. Download your Instagram data from Meta (`followers` + `following` JSON files)
2. Drop both files into the app
3. The app parses everything client-side and shows who doesn't follow you back

No uploads, no server, no API.  
Everything runs locally in your browser.

You can find FAQs and a tutorial directly on the website.
